Arawak Cay, Nassau

Water, gear, and open sky

Summaz Escape is at Arawak Cay, a quieter stretch of Nassau coastline just past the fish fry. Clear Caribbean water, real gear, no resort crowds.

Coming with the whole family? The Beach Access Package covers everyone who wants a proper beach day. Chairs and umbrella, kayaks, paddleboards, water scooters, snorkel masks, floaties, a water hammock, and bottled water all included. Great for kids and adults who just want to hang at the beach. Jet skis are not part of this one.

Want to add a jet ski ride for some of your group? Book those people on the Beach Access + Jet Ski Ride instead. They get all the beach stuff plus a 25-minute ride on the water. Adults take the wheel, and younger or less confident riders can hop on tandem at no extra charge. Need more time? Sessions can be extended on the day.

Want to add more action to your beach day? The Big Mable Tube Ride is available on-site for the whole group. Up to 6 riders pile onto a giant inflatable tube and get towed by a jet ski across open water. About 10 to 12 minutes. Kids 7 and up can join. $45 per person, paid on arrival.

Just here for the jet ski? The Jet Ski Rentals package skips the beach access and gets you straight on the water. Pick 30, 45, or 60 minutes. Adults book and ride, and if you're bringing along a younger rider or a family member, tandem is completely fine. Each jet ski seats two, no extra charge for the passenger.

Either way, you arrive between 10am and 6pm. No timed slots. No rush.

Common questions

What should I bring?

A towel, swimwear, and sunscreen. That's really all you need. Everything else is provided. No reef-safe requirement, but we'd appreciate it.

Can my child ride without an adult on the jet ski?

No. Riders under 16 must be on the back of an adult's jet ski. Book the child package for them. There's no minimum age for tandem riding, but the combined weight of both riders can't exceed 350 lb.

If a teenager (under 16) wants their own jet ski, they can book under the adult package with parent permission. Staff will confirm on arrival.

Do I need to pre-book or can I walk in?

We recommend booking in advance, especially on weekends and holidays. Walk-ins are welcome but subject to availability. Jet ski sessions are limited per day.

What time should I arrive?

Any time between 10am and 6pm works. We don't assign arrival slots. If you want to maximize your beach time, earlier is better. Last jet ski sessions go out well before 6pm.

What happens if it rains or the weather is bad?

We run in light rain and most conditions. If weather forces a cancellation, you'll get a full refund or can reschedule with no fee and no hassle. We'll contact you if we need to cancel on our end.

Can I leave valuables somewhere safe?

There's no secure storage on site. Leave phones, wallets, and keys locked in your vehicle or hotel before you arrive. We're not responsible for items left on the beach.

Is equipment shared between groups?

Beach equipment (kayaks, paddleboards, etc.) and jet skis are shared across groups on busy days. Our staff coordinate turns to keep things running smoothly. On quieter days, you'll often have gear to yourselves.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund if you cancel 7 or more days before your visit. No refund for cancellations within 7 days of the date.

To cancel, email booking@summazescape.com with your booking reference. Weather-related cancellations initiated by us are always fully refunded or rescheduled.
